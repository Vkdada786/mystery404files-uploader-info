Use and support

Mystery404files Uploader is a personal automation tool operated by GitHub user Vkdada786 for their own Drive folders and YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public sign-in or upload service.

Owner responsibilities

The owner selects queued videos, holds the rights and permissions needed to upload them, reviews titles and descriptions, declares the correct audience, and follows the rules of Google, YouTube and GitHub. Read YouTube's Terms of Service and this app's privacy policy.

Availability

Scheduled uploads depend on GitHub Actions availability and allowances, Google API limits, valid authorization and YouTube processing. A configured schedule does not guarantee an exact start time or successful publication. The owner should review workflow results and maintain backups of original content.

Support

Report non-sensitive issues through the public information repository. Never include credentials, token files, private video content or personal account information.
