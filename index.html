PERSONAL VIDEO AUTOMATION

Mystery404files Uploader

A personal tool for the owner of the Mystery404files YouTube channel. It uploads one queued video from the owner's Google Drive to the owner's YouTube channel using GitHub Actions.

What the app does

  1. Checks the configured Drive queue and the authorized YouTube channel.
  2. Downloads an original queued video into a temporary GitHub Actions workspace and uploads it to YouTube.
  3. Checks the upload status. After public processing is confirmed, moves the original from Pending to Published in Drive. The app does not delete Drive originals.

Video filenames supply upload titles. Upload IDs and queue stages are stored as private Drive file properties to avoid duplicate uploads.

This is an owner-operated personal app. This website provides information only; it has no Google sign-in, upload form, or access to account credentials. Facebook publishing is not currently enabled.

Google access and privacy

Google authorization permits access to Drive files and the YouTube channel for this workflow. Read the privacy policy for the data accessed, storage, sharing, retention and revocation options.
